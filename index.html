    WHAT THIS GUIDE IS ABOUT

    Good systems make their promises visible.

    01

    Know what must be true

    Translate product expectations into explicit rules, freshness limits, and durable outcomes.

    02

    Make change survivable

    Design writes, retries, messages, and projections for the messy timing of real networks.

    03

    Prepare to recover

    Keep the system understandable when it is slow, overloaded, or coming back from failure.
